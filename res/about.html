-About-
   

Network Service Placer is a tool, developed to facilitate the evaluations based on several network service placement strategies. It gives the opportunity to the researchers in this field of study to track the behaviour of the network service placement, by making adjustments in different aspects relating to substrate network, service graph and the placement algorithm, to see how the topology, node resources, link resources and QoS requirements influence the placement. In addition, it provides an infrastructure for the researchers to evaluate their own placement solutions. As long as their parameters are compatible, researchers can compare their results with the results obtained from our tool. It can also be used in other subjects relevant to this context (e.g. topology abstractions).
This tool is developed in b<>com, a private Technological Research Institute which explores, designs and provides innovations to companies that want to develop their competitiveness through digital technology.
